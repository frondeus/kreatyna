REC-07 · Ingredient lines with a name

The editor gets an Ingredients list under the canvas, and the read view lists the lines. This page works: add a line, type, drag a line by its handle onto another, remove one, and press Save to see the read view or "Name required".

1 · The editor

The list shows up to 6 lines, then it scrolls, so the canvas keeps its height. ＋ Line adds an empty line at the bottom and puts the cursor in it. You drag a line by ≡ onto another line: it takes that line's place (REC-07/Q1).

Decided

  • REC-07/Q1 Lines keep the order you added them; you drag one by its handle to reorder (E-09).
  • A name is trimmed and must not be empty. "Name required" shows from the first Save on.
  • Two lines may share a name. The − button removes a line at once; Cancel still asks about the whole edit.
  • If drag cannot work on macOS, each line gets Move Up and Move Down in its context menu (MAC-05/Q4).
