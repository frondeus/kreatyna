Decided

  • REC-08/Q1 The row shows a summary; a click opens the popover with Amount, Unit, Name and Note.
  • The amount is an exact decimal: "1.5" or "1,5", at most 6 decimal places, more than 0 (the catalog rules).
  • A unit needs an amount. An amount without a unit is fine ("2 eggs").
  • The note is optional and shows after the name, in grey.

Not in this slice

  • Custom units ("1 knob ginger"): REC-09.
  • The catalog link and its reference units: REC-10, REC-12.
  • Fractions such as "½".
