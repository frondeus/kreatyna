2 · Recipe editor: canvas MVP

A node editor, in the spirit of ComfyUI. Right-click on empty canvas to add a step. Drag from a step's output port (●, right side) to another step's input port (○, left side) to say "this comes after that". You place the nodes and the app remembers where they are. Solid nodes are active (hands busy); dashed nodes are waiting (hands free).

4 · Total time: one cook MVP

The list row's total time (D-15) is the one-cook estimate (D-29): your hands-on steps never overlap, and waiting steps run in the background. It covers the whole cook, prep included (D-33), so it tells you whether you have time for this recipe tonight. The MVP shows only the number; the timeline below explains it and is parked for later (F-13). Solid bars are active (hands busy); striped bars are waiting (hands free).

Roasted tomato pasta

For comparison: the longest chain would be 35 min (it assumes two pairs of hands), and the plain sum of all steps is 59 min.

Decided
  • D-41 This timeline view belongs in the real app, after the MVP (F-13). The MVP shows only the total.
  • D-29 One cook. D-33 Prep counts.
  • Parked: "Two cooks" mode (F-09); a pantry-aware total, e.g. leftover diced onion in the fridge skips a step (F-10).

Decided
  • D-27 One recipe at a time; several timers; Space marks the focused step done; progress survives quitting the app.
  • D-31 Cooking mode ships with timing. "Cook pasta" isn't offered at minute 8 just because nothing blocks it; it waits under "Coming up" until it's time to start.
  • D-42 Two kinds of timers in the bar: running (a step is in progress) and start (counts down to when a waiting step should begin).
  • D-43 Text size uses the standard ⌘+ / ⌘− and the View menu; no button in the window.
